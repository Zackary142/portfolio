My work

VoQuota: quoting made simple for tradespeople

Live product · co-founded

Tradespeople were losing jobs because writing quotes in Word or spreadsheets took hours. VoQuota lets them send a professional quote in minutes and turn it into an invoice with one click. I co-founded it with a fellow Virgin Media O2 intern, and we designed, built and launched it together.

  • Quotes that add up labour, materials and extras automatically
  • Customers accept or decline online, no account needed
  • The business's own logo and colours on every quote and invoice
  • Works on a phone, so quotes can be sent from the job
