VoQuota: quotes and invoices for tradespeople

Tradespeople lose jobs when quoting takes too long. Writing them in Word or a spreadsheet after a full day on site takes hours. VoQuota lets them build a quote on their phone in a few minutes, send it to the customer, and turn it into an invoice with one click once it's accepted.

I co-founded it with a fellow software engineering intern at Virgin Media O2. We designed, built and launched it between the two of us.
