VoQuota

Quoting and invoicing for tradespeople

Role
Co-founder, engineer
Status
Live

Tradespeople lose jobs when quoting takes too long, and writing quotes in Word after a day on site takes hours. VoQuota lets them build a quote on their phone in a few minutes, send it, and turn it into an invoice with one click once the customer accepts. I co-founded it with a fellow software engineering intern from Virgin Media O2, and we designed, built and launched it between the two of us.

  • Quote builder that totals labour, materials and extras, with VAT
  • Customer page to accept or decline, with no account needed
  • Each business's own branding on every quote, invoice and PDF
